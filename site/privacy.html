DOTX / Privacy

Privacy

Understand what the prototype stores and sends.

Local data

Browsing history, bookmarks and the search-engine selection are saved locally. Qt WebEngine may also store cookies, cache and other website data in its profile. The application has no private browsing mode or complete clear-browsing-data controls.

External services

Navigation and searches are sent to the websites and search provider you choose. Workspace links open third-party sites, which apply their own privacy policies. No DOTX cloud synchronization or provider API token storage is implemented.

Account disconnection

Sign out using each provider website. DOTX does not currently manage third-party account connections or revoke authorizations.
